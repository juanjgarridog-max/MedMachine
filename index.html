MedMachine

Combating Misinformation One Question at a Time

In service to our patients

Have you ever searched for medical information on Google that turned out to be incorrect?
Or haven't had time to go to the doctor because of a busy schedule?

In partnership with The Red Cross, we developed Med Machine.
Med Machine is an AI that, instead of having access to the entire internet, will only have access to medical information provided by doctors who are members of the American Red Cross organization.

How Does it Work?

What sets MedMachine apart from other AIs and a simple search engine is that it won't have access to the vast internet, which contains a lot of inaccurate information. Instead, we'll create a database of medical knowledge provided by real doctors, thus eliminating the problem of false medical information that creates misinformation.
